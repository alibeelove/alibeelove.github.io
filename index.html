MEET THE ARTIST

Alison Cauchi

An abstract artist inspired by the language of colour, emotion and the freedom of creative expression.

For Alison, painting is a personal journey — a way to explore thoughts and feelings through colour, texture and movement. Each canvas develops intuitively, allowing unexpected forms and combinations to emerge. The result is artwork that invites each viewer to find a meaning of their own.

Based in Malta, Alison continues to explore the possibilities of abstract art, creating pieces that celebrate individuality, imagination and the beauty of the unfinished story.
